Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Home Terms of Service

Shorts Studio is a personal desktop application. It is used only by the owner to create and publish short videos.

1. Data we collect

  • TikTok basic account information (such as username / open id) after you authorize Login Kit.
  • An access token so the app can upload videos you created to your own TikTok account.
  • Video files, scripts and captions generated on your computer.

2. How we use data

  • To show which TikTok account is connected.
  • To upload videos that you generate in the app to your TikTok profile, only when you choose to publish.

We do not sell data. We do not share data with advertisers. We do not use your data to train public models.

3. Where data is stored

Tokens and settings stay on your local computer (local database and local files). Videos go to TikTok only when you publish.

4. Third parties

  • TikTok / ByteDance, for login and video upload.
  • Stock media providers used to download background clips.
